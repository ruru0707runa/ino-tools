# Offer Compensation Reconciliation

## Rebuild the annual total from its parts

- List every pay component separately, with its monthly amount, its condition, and its bonus treatment, before you state any annual total.

- Multiply confirmed monthly pay by 12, and add bonus only for months the employer confirms in writing, with the eligibility rule for the first year stated beside it.

- Treat conditional allowances, attendance allowances, and staged allowances as unconfirmed until the trigger and the payment date are clear, and show the total with and without them.

- Compare offers by monthly pay as well as by annual total, because a lower monthly figure changes household cash flow even when bonus arithmetic looks close.

## Check period coverage before you add figures

- Identify the payer and the period covered by each withholding slip and each payslip before you add any two figures together.

- Never add a payslip period to a withholding total that already covers that period, and never treat a partial year slip as a full year figure.

- Surface a gap between documents instead of rounding it away, and name the missing period, the missing employer, or the unconfirmed bonus beside the gap.

- Ask for the payer name and the remarks column when a slip alone cannot show whether it covers one employer or several.

## Present the offer and negotiate in writing

- Present the employer offer, the current confirmed figure, and the gap in the same message, with each figure tied to its source document.

- Flag any clause that changes pay over time, such as a trial period, a staged allowance, or a first year bonus exclusion, before the user accepts.

- Put any negotiation or acceptance in writing after the user has reviewed the reconciled figures, and never treat a verbal reaction on a call as acceptance.

- Keep the source payslips distinct from the summary memo, and send only the periods the employer asked for unless the user approves more.

## Alternatives for better results

- Ask the employer for the monthly breakdown and bonus months in writing when a headline total is the only figure given.

- Recompute the total after any employer correction, rather than carrying an earlier total forward.

- Use a second employer offer only as a stated fact when negotiating, and name its source and status honestly.
